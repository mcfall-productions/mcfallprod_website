# Brief: McFall Productions, minimal direction

**Self-authored, not interviewed.** Scott's direction (2026-10-13): "a branch of the site that is much more minimal, inspired by apple.com". Everything else below is an authored decision, not something he said. Copy is reused from the review page in `../index.html`.

1. **Vibe:** quiet, exact, confident, monochrome with one gold. Reference: apple.com product pages.
2. **Journey:** claim, the buried catalog, one title rewritten, the proof (1,950), the services, working beside your team, production, the shows, the people, the free audit.
3. **Energy:** calm throughout. One loud moment, the proof.
4. **Feeling and peak:** see curve below.
5. **The one thing:** a catalog drawn as 1,950 dots that turn gold under your hand.
6. **Range:** premium-minimal, asked for by name.
7. **World:** distinct tiles with hard cuts between white, grey and black. No continuous world.
8. **Assets:** existing cover art and headshots only. Nothing generated.

**Grammar:** "Product keynote" (new). The unit is the tile: one statement, one sentence, at most two actions. Detail lives behind disclosures. A slim sticky ribbon carries the name, three anchors and the one CTA. Forbids: serifs, photographic grounds, scrub, pan, drift, magnet, spotlight, auto-opening dialogs, more than one accent.

**Hero depth:** three planes of bars (old episodes, old episodes found again, new episodes) that grow at different rates as the page scrolls. Scott asked for this graph back on 2026-10-13 in place of the first pass's dot field; the dots now appear only at the peak.

## Feeling curve
1. Clarity: one sentence on white, over a bar graph that grows as you start to scroll.
2. Recognition: "already recorded", plain statement, the ways a catalog gets buried on request.
3. Small surprise: a weak title is struck and a real one wipes in. Quiet, mostly white. This is the silence before the peak.
4. **Awe / proof (peak):** the page goes black, the number climbs to 1,950 and the dots turn gold as you scroll.
5. Confidence: three services as plain rows.
6. Relief: your team and ours, side by side.
7. Trust: real production figures.
8. Familiarity: the shows as an icon grid.
9. Warmth: the people.
10. Resolve: black again, one button, the audit.

**Peak, as a visitor would say it:** "the screen went black and a wall of grey dots turned gold while the number ran up to 1,950."
**Tell-someone sentence:** it's the site where you scroll and 1,950 dots light up, one for every episode title they rewrote.
**Authored silence:** the stats row space under the 1,950 label is empty until the count lands.
