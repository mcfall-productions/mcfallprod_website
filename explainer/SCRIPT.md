# BCG explainer: voiceover script

Edit `narration.json`, then run `./voice.sh` and `python3 build_timeline.py`.

**hook**
Every episode you've ever published is still out there. But the older ones get harder to find, every year.

**rise**
When old episodes are found again, their downloads climb. More downloads means more ad plays, which means more revenue from episodes you've already made.

**why**
Titles drift into four different formats. Early show notes are a single line. And hundreds of hours of conversation can't be searched.

**bcg**
Back Catalog Goldmine fixes that, across your whole catalog. Retitling, so people press play. Show notes, so episodes get found. And a transcript for every episode, on your podcast host and on your website, so search engines and listeners can find what was said. Better discoverability means more downloads.

**human**
Our automation writes the first draft in your brand's own style, guided by our extensive expertise in podcast discovery best practices. Then a U.S.-based editor on our team, fluent in English, checks each new piece of copy before anything goes live.

**publish**
And we don't hand you a document. We publish it ourselves, on your website and on your podcast host, whichever one you use. Every original is saved first.

**pays**
Here's why it pays. If your show uses dynamic ad insertion, a play of an old episode carries this month's ads. So when more people find your back catalog, you earn more from episodes you've already made.

**proof**
We did it first for The School of Greatness. Thirteen years of episodes. Nineteen hundred and fifty titles rewritten, and over eleven hundred hours of conversation made searchable.
