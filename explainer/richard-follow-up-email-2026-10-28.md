**What's covered**
Retitling, show notes and transcripts for the whole back catalog, published for the client on their podcast host and website, with every original saved so any episode can be put back. We price the audio feed and YouTube separately, because YouTube gets its own titles and descriptions written for search and the home feed. (Thumbnails aren't included.)

**Two review levels**
- *Reviewed:* our tool drafts, then a US-based native English speaker on our team reads every title and set of show notes. Guest names and their books or offers are checked in every transcript.
- *Automated:* same tool and same publishing, but the copy goes through automated checks instead of a full read: guest and book names against a list we build with the client, a scan for outdated or sensitive language, and length and duplicate checks. We spot-check a sample of every batch and read anything flagged.
