One note before the numbers. How much of a YouTube back catalog needs work varies a lot from channel to channel, so YouTube pricing is bespoke. The prices below are where we start, and we'll shape the final quote once we understand a client's channel on a walkthrough call.

**What's covered**
Retitling, show notes and transcripts for the whole back catalog, published for the client on their podcast host and website, with every original saved so any episode can be put back. YouTube is added on top, as described below.

**Two review levels**
- *Reviewed:* our tool drafts, then a US-based native English speaker on our team reads every title and set of show notes. Guest names and their books or offers are checked in every transcript.
- *Automated:* same tool and same publishing, but the copy goes through automated checks instead of a full read: guest and book names against a list we build with the client, a scan for outdated or sensitive language, and length and duplicate checks. We spot-check a sample of every batch and read anything flagged.

**YouTube add-ons: price per episode, starting at**

| | Reviewed | Automated |
|---|---|---|
| Titles and descriptions that mirror the audio feed | $2 | $2 |
| Titles and descriptions written separately for YouTube | $12 | $5 |
| Transcripts added to YouTube | $2 | $2 |

- *Mirrored copy* costs almost nothing extra, because the copy already exists and adding it to YouTube is automated.
- *Separate YouTube copy* costs more at either level. Reviewed adds a second read, since YouTube titles and descriptions need their own review apart from the audio feed ones. Automated still means our tool creating the new copy and putting it on YouTube.
- *Transcripts on YouTube* are automated, so they add very little.
- *Thumbnails* can be included for an additional cost. We'd set that price based on catalog size and what the client expects when it's done.

For comparison, freelancers charge roughly $25 to $150 for one set of show notes, and AI transcription alone runs about $15 an hour-long episode with no speaker labels.

**Turnaround (500 episodes):** Full Suite about 2 weeks Reviewed, 1 to 2 weeks Automated. 1,000 to 2,000 episodes take a few more weeks, never months. YouTube timing depends on how much of the channel a client wants done, so we'll confirm it after the call.

**Full Suite extras, no charge:** three months of monthly tune-ups, a written style guide for the client's team, and a 60-day comparison of the episodes we changed against the ones we left alone.

Everything starts with a free audit. We crawl the blog, the audio feed and, if they want, YouTube. No logins needed. The client gets a plain list of what's wrong, and the quote comes after a walkthrough call.

On results: we're promising discoverability, not a guaranteed lift in downloads. We don't have clean before and after numbers yet, and we'd rather say so up front.
