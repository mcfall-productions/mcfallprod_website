Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

McFall Productions makes podcasts easier to find. This page explains what information we collect on mcfallprod.com, why we collect it, who handles it for us, and the choices you have. McFall Productions is responsible for your information.

What we collect

  • Free audit requests: your show's name, your email address, whether you agreed to our newsletter, the wording you agreed to, when you agreed, and your browser's time zone.
  • Emails you send us: your address and whatever you choose to include.
  • Site usage: if your browser is set to a US time zone, Google Analytics records things like the pages you visit, your device and browser, your approximate location, and how you reached the site. It uses cookies to do this.

How we use it

  • To prepare and email the audit you asked for, and to reply to you. We do this because you requested it.
  • To send our weekly newsletter, if you agreed to receive it. We rely on your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time.
  • To keep a record of that consent, which we need in order to comply with email marketing laws.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.

We use your browser's time zone only to decide which newsletter sign-up option to show you and whether to load Google Analytics. Visitors outside the United States can get the free audit without joining the newsletter, and Google Analytics doesn't load for them.

Who handles it for us

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with services that help us run the site and our email:

  • Web3Forms delivers the audit form to our inbox.
  • Google provides the web fonts on our homepage and, for visitors in US time zones, Google Analytics.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Our email providers store and send our messages, including the newsletter.

Links to services such as Calendly, YouTube and Apple Podcasts take you to their sites, where their own privacy policies apply. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Where it's stored

We're based in the United States, and your information may be processed there or wherever our service providers operate. Those countries may have different data protection laws from yours.

How long we keep it

We keep audit requests and related emails for as long as we need them to work with you. We keep you on the newsletter until you unsubscribe. After that, we keep only what we need to honor your choice and show that we had your consent. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe: use the link in any newsletter, or email team@mcfallprod.com.
  • Analytics: if you're in a US time zone, block cookies in your browser, or use Google's opt-out add-on.
  • Your data: depending on where you live, including the EU, UK and Canada, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or move your information, to object to how we use it, and to withdraw consent. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect anything we did before you withdrew it.
  • Complaints: please contact us first. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email us and we'll respond within the time your local law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
