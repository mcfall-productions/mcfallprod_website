Who to introduce us to

  • The host, if you know them
  • The show's producer, often the best person to start with
  • Whoever runs the business side: a manager, partner or head of operations

Signs the timing is right

  • They just passed a milestone, like episode 500 or 1,000
  • The show has been renamed or rebranded along the way
  • They've moved to a new podcast host
  • They run ads or sponsors on the show

Not for us right now: newer shows with fewer than 500 episodes, and shows owned by a large network such as iHeart, SiriusXM or Wondery, where these decisions are made centrally. Not sure about a show? Send us its name and we'll take a look.

How it works

  1. You introduce us

    One short email to your contact with us copied in. The wording is below, ready to paste.

  2. We confirm it's yours

    We reply to you by email to confirm the introduction counts as your referral, so there's never a question later.

  3. They get their free audit

    We send the show its free audit within 48 hours and offer a short call to walk through it.

  4. They sign, you get paid

    If the show signs within six months of your intro, we pay you 20% of its first project, with no cap, once the project is paid for.
